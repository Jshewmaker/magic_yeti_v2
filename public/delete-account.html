Delete your Magic Yeti account

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Magic Yeti, the app by Yeti Productions (Joshua Shewmaker).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to yetiproductions2019@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Magic Yeti account".
  3. Send the email from the email address you use to sign in, or include that address in the email.

We delete your account within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

Data that we delete

  • Your sign-in account (email, Google, or Apple sign-in)
  • Your profile: username, display name, and profile picture
  • Your match history and your commander decks
  • Your friends list, friend requests, and blocked users
  • Records in other users' friends lists that point to you

Data that we keep

  • Games that you played with other users stay in their match history. These records show the player name that was used in that game.
  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics data, which does not identify you.

We keep no other copies of your data after deletion. Backups are deleted within 30 days.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.
